You may copy, adapt and redistribute the data under the ODbL, including its
attribution and applicable share-alike/source-availability requirements. Retain
this notice with redistributed datasets. This machine-readable JSON is the
complete OSM-derived directory used by this batch, with original source IDs,
retained tags, coordinates and provenance. No user account or inventory data is
included in this redistributable directory. This notice covers this data, not a
claim of ownership of third-party names/trademarks or a change to app code licensing.

Data extraction providers: Geofabrik (New York and Georgia) and BBBike (Chicago,
Dallas, Denver and Los Angeles). The underlying data is OpenStreetMap; provider
URLs, snapshot timestamps and SHA-256 hashes are retained in each record and in
`osm-us-metros-review.json`. Dates are source snapshot dates, not proof that a shop
is currently operating. FoodBuddy records remain independently unverified.

The Nearby Stores map includes a visible OpenStreetMap attribution link, separate
from Apple Maps' own attribution. List views include the data attribution, and
store details expose source URLs and review/snapshot dates.
