# Official manufacturer photographs — 20-product pilot

Reviewed November 19, 2026. **20 official product pages match the stored barcode and
named variant; 20 primary product image URLs are recorded. Sixteen also corroborate
package size through the page's image description or product title. Four Jimmy Dean
items still need image/package-size review. Zero images have an established license
for FoodBuddy's bundled redistribution; all 20 require permission.**

No official image was downloaded, added to the registry, or substituted for an
existing photograph. Existing ten enabled photos, credits and neutral placeholders
remain unchanged. Recorded URLs are research references, not runtime requests.

The complete per-product URLs, GTINs, product/image descriptions, identity limits,
terms URLs, permission status and page hashes are in

## Licensing findings

- [Hershey](https://www.thehersheycompany.com/en_us/home/terms-and-conditions.html)
  limits the ordinary download allowance to personal use; redistribution needs consent.
- [General Mills](https://www.generalmills.com/privacy-security/legal-terms/us-english-terms-of-use),
  linked by Cheerios, requires express consent for distribution of graphics. A public
  product-image URL or attribution is not an app license.
- [McCormick](https://www.mccormick.com/pages/terms-and-conditions) allows individual
  noncommercial use and requires written authorization for other distribution. It
  also restricts automated collection; no further collection or bulk image download
  is proposed without authorization.
- [Tyson](https://www.tysonfoods.com/legal/terms-of-use) restricts redistribution,
  systematic collection and incorporation of its content. No applicable app-image
  permission was established for Jimmy Dean.

School-project status is not treated as a blanket redistribution exception. Existing
Open Food Facts licenses do not authorize copying separate manufacturer assets.
No permission request was sent to any company. Next step: obtain written permission
covering download, offline bundling, distribution to students/instructors or app users,
permitted resizing/cropping, attribution and duration; then visually inspect only the
authorized assets. Until then, leave current photos/placeholders intact.
