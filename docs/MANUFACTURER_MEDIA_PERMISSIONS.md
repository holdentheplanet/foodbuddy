## Permission request scope

Supply the existing pilot's exact product names, sizes, barcodes, product URLs and image
URLs. Ask whether the owner permits downloading and bundling those assets in FoodBuddy,
a school grocery/allergy app; distribution to instructors, classmates and any app users;
offline display; resizing/cropping; required credit; expiration and territory. Distinguish
classroom-only demonstration from publishing an app or source repository containing images.
Retain the written grant and asset-specific restrictions before changing the image registry.

**Result: zero of the 20 exact product images newly cleared for FoodBuddy bundling.**
McCormick has the clearest published educational provision, whose applicability still
requires clarification. Tyson offers reusable gallery material, but no exact pilot SKU
was identified there. Hershey and General Mills provide inquiry routes, not a granted
license. This is a documented permission review, not an assumption that educational use
or attribution alone establishes authorization. Current approved photos and credits remain intact.
